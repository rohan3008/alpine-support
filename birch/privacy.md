# Birch — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 22 October 2026_

Birch (a sauna & cold plunge rounds timer, from the developer of
[Alpy](https://alpy.uk)) collects nothing.

- **Your protocols and session log** are stored only on your device.
  They never leave it — Birch makes **no network connections at all**.
- **Notifications** are scheduled locally on your device so phase
  changes reach you while the phone is locked. Nothing is sent to or
  from any server.
- No account, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, no third-party SDKs.
- No data is collected, so none can be sold or shared.

Delete the app and everything it stored goes with it.

# Birch — Cold Plunge & Sauna Timer

Birch times contrast rounds — sauna heat, cold plunge, rest — and
logs what you actually did. Big numerals you can read through steam,
locked-phone alerts at every transition, streaks and honest totals
(skipped time never counts).

No account. No ads. No health claims. **No network connections at
all** — your protocols and log live on your phone and nowhere else.

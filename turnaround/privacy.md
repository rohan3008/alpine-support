# Turnaround — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

Turnaround (a live turn-back time for hikers, ski tourers and anyone out on the hill, from the
developer of [Alpy](https://alpy.uk)) collects nothing.

- **Your trips, GPS tracks, pace and settings** are stored only on your device. Deleting the app
  deletes them. We have no copy.
- **Everything is worked out on your phone**: dusk, your way home and your turn-back time.
  Turnaround makes no network requests for your trips.
- **Notifications** are scheduled on the phone itself. No push server is involved.
- **No account, no sign-in, no email collected. No analytics, no ads, no tracking.**

**Location.** Turnaround asks for location "Always" so the turn-back time stays current with the
phone in your pocket. Background location runs only while a trip is in progress, and iOS shows the
location indicator while it does. Your location never leaves the phone. You can change this any
time in Settings → Turnaround → Location.

**Children.** Turnaround is not aimed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

If a future version adds optional features that need a server (for example, weather forecasts),
this page will say exactly what is sent before that version ships, and the date above will change.

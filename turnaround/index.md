# Turnaround — Turn-Back Timer

**Every mountain has a time. The summit is optional. Getting back is not.**

Turnaround shows one number while you're out: **the time to turn back** to get home before dark.
It works it out live from your own pace, your recorded trail reversed, and civil dusk, all on your
phone, with no account and no signal needed.

Every time is an estimate. Use your own judgement, carry a map, and follow local guidance.

# Alpy — the free, private ski app

**Alpy ski app** for iPhone, launching for the 2026/27 season. Every
feature free, no account, no ads — and your GPS never leaves the
phone, because there is no server for it to go to.

- **Every run races your own best** — personal records per named run,
  "−0:17 vs best" verdicts, and a race chart showing where the time went.
- **Ski Debrief** — per-run time, drop, speeds, estimated turns,
  course, airtime, and a day-rhythm fatigue read. Estimates labeled.
- **Day replay** — scrub your actual line across real 3D terrain.
- **Trail maps two ways** — schematic piste chart + 3D view from
  OpenStreetMap, with your live position while tracking. Search
  [3,652 ski areas worldwide](resorts/), 51 of them hand-verified.
- **Powder alerts, freeze curve, official avalanche bulletins** (SLF,
  avalanche.org) — with an honest "no data" state, never a guess.
- **AI on the device, full stop** — morning report and day summary
  by Apple Intelligence. No keys, no cloud AI, no account.

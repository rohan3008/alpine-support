# Alpine — Support

Alpine is a free, privacy-first ski companion for iPhone: GPS day
tracking with a 3D Day Replay, live summit weather, official avalanche
bulletins, and OpenStreetMap trail maps for 51 resorts.

- **Privacy policy:** [privacy](privacy)
- **Questions, bugs, feature requests:** email
  [rohanshah90@gmail.com](mailto:rohanshah90@gmail.com) or
  [open an issue](https://github.com/rohan3008/alpine-support/issues).

The app has no account, no ads, and collects nothing — so if something
breaks, your email or issue report is the only way we find out. Thank
you.

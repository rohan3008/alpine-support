# Alpy — the free, private ski app

**Alpy ski app** for iPhone, launching for the 2026/27 season. Every
feature free, no account, no ads — and your GPS never leaves the
phone, because there is no server for it to go to.

**And underneath it all:** GPS day tracking with barometric fusion and
honest error bars, lift-line estimates per lift, airtime and turn-load
estimates (labeled), search across
[3,652 ski areas worldwide](resorts/) — every feature free, forever.

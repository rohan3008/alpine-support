Privacy policy

Slalo: Ski Technique Lessons · Last updated 10 November 2026

Slalo collects no data. This policy covers the Slalo iPhone app and this website.

The app

  • Slalo works fully offline. It makes no network requests, so nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere. Your lesson progress stays on your iPhone (see below).
  • There is no account, no sign-in and nothing to register.
  • There are no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and no third-party services or SDKs.
  • The app asks for no permissions: no location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, motion or notifications.
  • There are no in-app purchases and no ads.

Your progress

Slalo remembers which steps you've ticked, which quizzes you've passed and which ski pins you've earned. It keeps this on your iPhone only, in the standard storage iOS gives every app for its settings (UserDefaults). Slalo never sends it anywhere, and I can't see it. If you back up your iPhone, the backup includes it along with your other app data. To delete it, open About from the home screen and choose Reset progress, or delete the app.

The App Store privacy label for Slalo is "Data Not Collected", and that matches this policy.

Apple

Apple handles App Store downloads and may give developers anonymous, aggregated figures such as download counts and crash reports from people who have chosen to share them with developers. These come from Apple's systems, under Apple's privacy policy. Slalo itself adds nothing to them.

This website

These pages are plain HTML with no cookies, analytics, fonts or scripts from anyone else. The company hosting the pages may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own policy.

Email

If you email me, I use your address and message only to reply, and I don't share them with anyone.

Children

Slalo collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date before the app changes.

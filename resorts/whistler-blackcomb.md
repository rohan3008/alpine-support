# Whistler Blackcomb

*Canada · one of the 51 catalog resorts in [Alpy](/), the free ski app, with coordinates and base elevation checked against live geocoding and terrain-elevation data.*

Whistler Blackcomb, in Canada, has lift-served terrain from 675 m at the base to 2,284 m, a 1,609 m vertical. OpenStreetMap volunteers have mapped about 322 km of downhill pistes and 35 passenger lifts within 7 km of the base area; that is a map count, not the resort's advertised figure. For the 2026/27 season it is covered by Epic.

## Elevation and vertical

- **Base:** 675 m
- **Summit:** 2,284 m
- **Lift-served vertical:** 1,609 m
- **Coordinates:** 50.1163, -122.9574 (village/base area, verified against live geocoding and terrain elevation)

## Pistes on the map

These numbers count what volunteers have drawn on OpenStreetMap within 7 km of Whistler Blackcomb's base-area coordinates, as baked into Alpy on 4 November 2026. They are **not** Whistler Blackcomb's official piste kilometres: OpenStreetMap measures each mapped piste line, can include runs from neighbouring areas inside that circle, and misses anything nobody has mapped yet. Piste areas drawn as closed outlines are left out, so their edges are not counted as run length.

# Cortina d'Ampezzo

*Italy · one of the 51 catalog resorts in [Alpy](/), the free ski app. Coordinates and base elevation are checked against live geocoding and terrain-elevation data.*

Cortina d'Ampezzo, in Italy, has lift-served terrain from 1,224 m at the base to 2,932 m, a 1,708 m vertical. OpenStreetMap volunteers have mapped about 71 km of downhill pistes and 24 passenger lifts within 7 km of the base area. That is a map count, not the resort's own figure. For the 2026/27 season it is covered by Ikon (limited days).

## Elevation and vertical

- **Base:** 1,224 m
- **Summit:** 2,932 m
- **Lift-served vertical:** 1,708 m
- **Coordinates:** 46.5405, 12.1357 (village/base area)

## Pistes on the map

These numbers count what volunteers have drawn on OpenStreetMap within 7 km of Cortina d'Ampezzo's base-area coordinates, as baked into Alpy on 4 November 2026. They are not Cortina d'Ampezzo's official piste kilometres. OpenStreetMap measures each mapped piste line, can include runs from neighbouring areas inside that circle, and misses anything nobody has mapped yet. Piste areas drawn as closed outlines are left out, so their edges do not count as run length.

- **Downhill pistes mapped:** about 71 km
- **Named runs:** 57
- **Passenger lifts mapped:** 24

# Mammoth Mountain

*United States · one of the 51 hand-verified resorts in [Alpy](/), the free ski app.*

Mammoth Mountain, in the United States, has lift-served terrain from 2,424 m at the base to 3,369 m, a 945 m vertical. OpenStreetMap volunteers have mapped about 101 km of downhill pistes and 28 passenger lifts within 7 km of the base area; that is a map count, not the resort's advertised figure. For the 2026/27 season it is covered by Ikon.

## Elevation and vertical

- **Base:** 2,424 m
- **Summit:** 3,369 m
- **Lift-served vertical:** 945 m
- **Coordinates:** 37.6485, -118.9721 (village/base area, verified against live geocoding and terrain elevation)

## Pistes on the map

These numbers count what volunteers have drawn on OpenStreetMap within 7 km of Mammoth Mountain's base-area coordinates, as baked into Alpy on 4 November 2026. They are **not** Mammoth Mountain's official piste kilometres: OpenStreetMap measures each mapped piste line, can include runs from neighbouring areas inside that circle, and misses anything nobody has mapped yet. Piste areas drawn as closed outlines are left out, so their edges are not counted as run length.

- **Downhill pistes mapped:** about 101 km

- **Passenger lifts mapped:** 28

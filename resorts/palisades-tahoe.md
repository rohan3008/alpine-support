# Palisades Tahoe

*United States · one of the 51 catalog resorts in [Alpy](/), the free ski app. Coordinates and base elevation are checked against live geocoding and terrain-elevation data.*

Palisades Tahoe, in the United States, has lift-served terrain from 1,890 m at the base to 2,758 m, a 868 m vertical. OpenStreetMap volunteers have mapped about 120 km of downhill pistes and 44 passenger lifts within 7 km of the base area. That is a map count, not the resort's own figure. For the 2026/27 season it is covered by Ikon.

## Elevation and vertical

- **Base:** 1,890 m
- **Summit:** 2,758 m
- **Lift-served vertical:** 868 m
- **Coordinates:** 39.1969, -120.2358 (village/base area)

## Pistes on the map

These numbers count what volunteers have drawn on OpenStreetMap within 7 km of Palisades Tahoe's base-area coordinates, as baked into Alpy on 4 November 2026. They are not Palisades Tahoe's official piste kilometres. OpenStreetMap measures each mapped piste line, can include runs from neighbouring areas inside that circle, and misses anything nobody has mapped yet. Piste areas drawn as closed outlines are left out, so their edges do not count as run length.

- **Downhill pistes mapped:** about 120 km
- **Named runs:** 135
- **Passenger lifts mapped:** 44

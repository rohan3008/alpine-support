Privacy policy

Pisteway: Ski Navigation · Last updated 18 November 2026

Pisteway collects no data. This policy covers the Pisteway iPhone app and this website.

The app

  • There is no account, no sign-in and nothing to register.
  • There are no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • There are no in-app purchases and no ads.

Location

Pisteway uses your location on your iPhone to suggest the nearest resort and to guide you along your route. While a route is active it keeps using location with the screen locked, so the voice can carry on, and iOS shows the blue location indicator. Your location is never sent to me or to anyone else.

Camera

When you hold the phone up, the camera shows a live view behind the direction chevrons. Pisteway takes no photos or video, and nothing from the camera is stored or sent anywhere.

Maps and piste data

  • Maps are drawn by Mapbox. Pisteway turns Mapbox telemetry off. Loading map tiles sends normal web requests to Mapbox, as any map does, and the Mapbox software sends Mapbox an anonymous usage count that Mapbox uses for billing. Pisteway never gives Mapbox your location. See Mapbox's privacy policy.
  • Piste and lift data comes from OpenStreetMap. 51 resorts are built into the app. For other ski areas, Pisteway downloads the data from the public Overpass API at overpass-api.de, or from the VK Maps Overpass mirror (maps.mail.ru) if the first one is busy. The request contains the resort's coordinates, not yours. Like any web request, it reaches those servers from your internet address.

Settings stored on your iPhone

Pisteway remembers your last resort, your skiing level, whether voice is muted and whether you've seen the safety note. It keeps these on your iPhone only, in the standard storage iOS gives every app for its settings. Downloaded piste maps are kept on the phone too, so they open faster next time. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

The App Store privacy label for Pisteway is "Data Not Collected", and that matches this policy.

Apple

Apple handles App Store downloads and may give developers anonymous, aggregated figures such as download counts and crash reports from people who have chosen to share them with developers. These come from Apple's systems, under Apple's privacy policy. Pisteway itself adds nothing to them.

This website

These pages are plain HTML with no cookies, analytics, fonts or scripts from anyone else. The company hosting the pages may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own policy.

Email

If you email me, I use your address and message only to reply, and I don't share them with anyone.

Children

Pisteway collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date before the app changes.

# Alpy vs Slopes

Both are iPhone ski-tracking apps. The short version: **Slopes puts
its per-run analytics, 3D maps and replay behind a Premium
subscription; everything comparable in Alpy is free** — and Alpy adds
things Slopes doesn't list at any price. Slopes is genuinely better at
some things too; they're at the bottom, because a comparison that
concedes nothing isn't worth your trust. (Checked against Slopes'
App Store listing, October 2026.)

| | Alpy (free, all of it) | Slopes |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Free. No premium tier, no account, no ads | Free core; per-run analytics, interactive maps, fitness insights need a Premium subscription |
| Per-run metrics | Time, drop, top/avg speed, estimated turns, course, airtime — free | Per-run performance analytics is a Premium feature |
| Racing yourself | Personal records on every named run, "−0:17 vs best" verdicts, race chart overlaying today vs your fastest | Best-run highlights (Premium); no attempt-vs-attempt race chart listed |
| Powder alerts | Background alerts when fresh snow crosses your threshold | Not listed |
| Avalanche | Official SLF + avalanche.org bulletins, honest "no bulletin" state | Not listed |
| AI | On-device Apple Intelligence morning report and day summary — no cloud, no keys | None |
| Trail maps | OSM schematic chart + 3D view, free, live position while tracking; 3,652 OSM ski areas searchable | Licensed interactive maps for 2,000+ resorts — Premium |
| Day replay | Your line scrubbed across 3D terrain — free | Tied to Premium maps |
| Privacy | No server exists: GPS, motion and history never leave the phone | Privacy-focused policy; friends/trips/sync run through their backend |

## What Slopes does better

- **Friends across the mountain** — server-based live location beats
  Alpy's Bluetooth-proximity Buddy Beacon for finding your crew.
- **Official-quality maps** — licensed resort data with facilities
  (bathrooms, ski patrol); Alpy's OSM charts are honest but plainer.
- **Live lift & trail status** for 50+ North American resorts
  (Premium). Alpy refuses to fake this without an honest source.
- **Apple Watch app and years of polish**, a huge community, Android
  support, trips and social features.

If those matter most to you, use Slopes — it's a fine app. If you
want the full instrument panel free, private and honest about its
estimates, that's [Alpy](./).

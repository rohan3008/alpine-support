Trail Finder

Pick a run, point your phone at the mountain, and an arrow over the live camera swings toward it — "To your left · ~2.9 km".

Questions skiers ask

Is Alpy really free?

Yes. Every feature is free: there are no ads, no account and no in-app purchases. Day tracking, personal records, the 3D map, day replay, powder alerts and avalanche bulletins all work without paying anything.

Does Alpy upload my location?

No. Your GPS tracks, records and replays are stored only on your iPhone, and there is no Alpy server to send them to. Weather and avalanche requests use the resort's coordinates, not yours; map tiles are downloaded for the area on screen, like any map app.

Does Alpy work without phone signal?

Day tracking does. GPS and the barometer need no data connection, and every run is saved on the phone. Live conditions, avalanche bulletins and new map areas need a connection to load.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Open the app and it works.

Which ski resorts does Alpy cover?

You can search 3,652 ski areas worldwide from OpenStreetMap, with piste maps where mappers have drawn them. Official avalanche bulletins come from SLF in Switzerland and avalanche.org in the US; where no official bulletin exists, the app says so.

Is the AI summary sent to the cloud?

No. The morning report and day summary are written by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. It only rephrases your measured numbers, and phones without Apple Intelligence get a plain written summary instead.

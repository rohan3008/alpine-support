# Alpy: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

Alpy (formerly Alpine) is built to collect nothing. It has no accounts, no
server of ours and no analytics.

## What the app stores on your device

- **Your rider profile** (height, weight, ability, style), **resort list** and
  **unit preference**, saved as a small file on your device only.
- **GPS ski-day tracks** (Day Tracker, Day Replay, Ski Debrief), recorded only
  while you have tracking switched on and written only to the app's private
  storage. They are never uploaded; there is no server to upload them to.
- **Motion data**: while you track a day, the barometer and motion sensors are
  used on the device to measure altitude and estimate airtime and turn load.
- **Favourites and day plans**: runs and places you star, and day plans you
  make, stay on your device.
- **Condition snapshots, trail maps, places and holiday calendars** are cached
  on your device so the app works offline.

## AI

- Text written in the app (the morning report, the day summary and the run
  suggestion line) is generated entirely on your device by Apple's on-device
  model, or by a simple template on devices without Apple Intelligence.
  Nothing is sent to any AI service.
- The resort guides were written ahead of time with an AI model and are
  bundled inside the app. They are labelled as AI wherever they appear.

## Optional features

- **Camera (Trail Finder):** shows your camera as a live viewfinder while an
  arrow points toward a run you picked. No photo or video is captured, stored,
  analysed or transmitted; the camera session has no recording output.
- **Buddy Beacon (Bluetooth):** off until you switch it on. While it is on and
  the app is open, it broadcasts the name you type to phones nearby that also
  have the beacon on, so you can find each other. Nothing else is shared, and
  nothing is stored or sent over the internet.
- **Powder alerts (notifications):** off until you switch them on. The app
  checks the snowfall forecast for your resort in the background and shows a
  notification on your device. Nothing is sent about you.
- **Sharing a day plan:** only when you tap Share does the plan leave the app,
  through the standard iOS share sheet, to whoever you choose. If you choose
  Save Image, the picture is added to your photos; the app never reads your
  photo library.

## What the app does NOT do

- No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers.
- No accounts, no sign-in, no backend.
- No personal data is collected, sold or shared. There is nothing to sell.

## Network requests the app makes

To show live information, the app contacts these services directly from your
device: Open-Meteo (weather), WSL Institute SLF and Avalanche.org (avalanche
bulletins), OpenStreetMap's Overpass API (trail geometry and named places near
the resort), OpenHolidays and Nager.Date (public and school holiday calendars
for the crowd forecast; requests contain only a country code and dates), and
the public RSS feeds of ski publications. Requests contain resort coordinates
or country codes, never your identity. Each service's own privacy policy
governs its handling of requests.

The 3D terrain map is rendered with the Mapbox Maps SDK, which fetches map
tiles from Mapbox's servers (those requests carry the map area being viewed
and the app's access token). Mapbox's telemetry collection is switched off by
the app using the SDK's own opt-out setting, and the map's ⓘ attribution
button shows Mapbox's terms and that same opt-out control.
